How it works

From the kabadi lane to authorised recycling

A walk through one lot of scrap: who uses which channel, what the platform checks, and what each person gets out of it.

Step 1 · Reach

Four channels, one price

The same rules-based price engine feeds every channel, so a picker without a smartphone hears the same fair rate as a kabadiwala with one.

  • IVR line for waste pickers on feature phones: press a number, hear the rate in Hindi.
  • Printed price boards at scrap markets, refreshed from the same engine.
  • WhatsApp bot: send a material name, get the rate and the nearest recyclers.
  • This website for kabadiwalas, recyclers, ULBs and brands.

Steps 2–5 · One lot, end to end

What happens to a kilo of circuit boards

2. Capture & classify

The kabadiwala photographs the lot. A small TensorFlow.js model runs in the browser and suggests the category. It only suggests: he confirms, or picks from the list. He enters the weight from his own kanta, or says it aloud in Hindi.

3. Value & match

He sees the best authorised bid, the typical local rate and the difference in rupees. A live map ranks CPCB-authorised recyclers by rate, distance and pickup slot. He books a pickup, with cash as the default.

4. Verified handover

At pickup, the photo, GPS, time and weight are hashed with SHA-256 and linked to the previous record, making the ledger tamper-evident. The recycler weighs the lot and counter-confirms. A difference above 5% goes to the admin as a dispute.

5. Record & report

The sale is written into the kabadiwala's ledger and feeds his Earnings Passport. Recyclers export EPR-ready records, and ULBs see a ward-wise registry of workers and tonnes diverted.

Trust & inclusion

Designed for low-literacy users and low-end phones

Works on weak networks

Pages are light, data is kept on the device, and the IVR needs no data at all.

Every AI step has a fallback

If the model can't load, the kabadiwala picks the material from a list. Nothing depends on AI.

Hazard guidance

CRT, battery and circuit-board lots show picture and audio safety steps before pickup.

Audio everywhere

सुनें buttons read each page aloud in Hindi. Weight can be spoken instead of typed.

Cash-first

Cash is the default payment. UPI and AePS are optional, and the ledger records all of them.

DPDP Act 2023

Consent first, optional e-Shram and Aadhaar link, minimal retention, and no personal data on public pages.

Technology

A plain multi-page website with no framework and no build step. It deploys to any static host.

SHA-256 hash chain
