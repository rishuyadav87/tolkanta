Why we built it

India generated about 14.1 lakh tons of e-waste in FY 2025-26. Independent estimates say 85–95% of it is still collected and dismantled informally. Formal recycling capacity exists; the missing link is a fair, safe and traceable route from the kabadi lane to the recycler.

Recyclers and brands need verified material for their EPR targets (70% now, 80% from 2027-28). Kabadiwalas need a fair price and a record. Cities need data on their informal workforce. TolKanta connects the three.
